Myofascial Release Massage

Myofascial release massage is a therapeutic technique that focuses on releasing tension, stickiness and tightness in the fascia, which is the connective tissue that surrounds our muscles, bones and organs. This type of massage aims to alleviate pain, improve flexibility, and enhance range of motion by applying gentle pressure and stretching to the affected areas. It is commonly used to treat chronic pain, fibromyalgia and other fascial disorders.
